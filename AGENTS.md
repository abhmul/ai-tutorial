# AGENTS.md

- Audience: newcomers with no AI experience. Plain language; define terms.
- Tone: neutral and factual. No superlatives, hype, slogans, or emoji.
- Capability claims must trace to official documentation.
- Cautionary claims must come from a primary or peer-reviewed source, cited in
  `references.md`, not inline.
- DOIs, arXiv IDs, and figures in `references.md` were fact-checked — verify
  before changing them.
- Keep the guide and one-pager in sync: same claims, same sources.
- Do not hard-code model version names unless we are giving specific examples.
- Use inline markdown links to direct readers to further reading.
