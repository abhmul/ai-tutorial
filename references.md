# References

Sources backing the claims in `ai-tools-guide.md` and `ai-tools-onepager.md`,
independently fact-checked against primary sources.

**Capabilities, models, interfaces.** Anthropic, Claude Code documentation:
[overview](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/overview);
[best practices](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/best-practices) — plan mode and
the "trust-then-verify gap"; [permission modes](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/permission-modes)
— permission prompts.

**Citation fabrication.** Walters, W. H., & Wilder, E. I. (2023). Fabrication
and errors in the bibliographic citations generated by ChatGPT. *Scientific
Reports* 13:14045. DOI: [10.1038/s41598-023-41032-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-41032-5) — GPT-3.5 fabricated 55% of
citations, GPT-4 18%; 24–43% of the non-fabricated citations still had errors.

**Sycophancy.** Sharma et al. (2023). Towards Understanding Sycophancy in
Language Models. arXiv:[2310.13548](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.13548) (Anthropic; published at ICLR 2024,
peer-reviewed). — Leading assistants consistently exhibit sycophancy; humans
and preference models sometimes favor convincing but incorrect answers.

**Confabulation.** Farquhar et al. (2024). Detecting hallucinations in large
language models using semantic entropy. *Nature* 630:625–630.
DOI: [10.1038/s41586-024-07421-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07421-0) — Confident, arbitrary, incorrect generations
that change across re-asking.
